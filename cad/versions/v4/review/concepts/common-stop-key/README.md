# Common original T/V key

Option A is promoted to V4: the unchanged key moves with the plunger through
an integral 9.2 mm raised seat, while the closed travel slot is in the plate.
V arms spread across the slot, not along it, catching 0.8 mm per side.
The T rests on a continuous 7 mm wide seat with support-avoiding ramps.
Remove the key for end-on plunger insertion. Free pull is 12.6 mm.

PETG bracket/plunger and original TPU key slices have zero support/interface
paths with automatic supports ON and bridge suppression OFF. Sampled end-on
insertion without key and operating travel pass. Positive escape obstructions
are measured beyond the stop and when the rigid key is pulled upward.
Physical TPU insertion, recovery, loaded retention and wear remain untested.
See `../../common-key-validation.json`. No print sent.

Option B remains a rejected reference: its fixed fork blocks insertion,
with 22.56 mm³ intersection at 17 mm insertion displacement.
