A · Square press-stud

Recommended. Overlap the ends and press a square peg through a square hole. A small lip holds it; peel the tab back to release.

Press perpendicular to the overlapping tabs. A rounded peg root and corners let the TPU deform. The latch stays outside the neck-contact surface.

B · Tongue into a short sleeve

Slide a flat tongue into the other end. A small bump engages a window in the sleeve; lift the window edge to release.

Insertion runs along the strap. The sleeve guides the tongue, but a soft TPU tongue may be harder to feed through than the press-stud.
