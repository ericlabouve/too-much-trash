Actual TPU toolpaths + supports

A · Square press-stud

Overlap the ends by 6 mm and press the tapered square peg through the square hole. Peel the receiving end back to release.

0 g support / interface · automatic supports enabled

3 mm square hole · 2.6 mm peg · 3.2 mm retaining lip. Both ends retain the band width; no large paddles.

B · Tongue into short sleeve

Feed the end through a 6 mm sleeve. A small bump enters the release window; flex the sleeve upward to withdraw the tongue.

0 g support / interface · automatic supports enabled

The outside is rectangular; the internal roof slopes to a peak to avoid a flat unsupported ceiling. Sleeve width 8.8 mm.

Both bands print flat, then bend around the neck. The 73.6 mm developed length targets a 12.8 × 17.8 mm inside opening with a short overlap; actual bend radius and stretch may require adjustment. A 4 mm string eye sits on one side and can be flipped to the opposite side.

These are review concepts, not a promotion into V3. The seated ends pass rigid interference checks, but insertion, wrapping, retention and TPU creep are untested. No print will be sent.
